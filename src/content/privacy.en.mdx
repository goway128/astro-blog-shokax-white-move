This site respects and is committed to protecting the privacy of every visitor. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected during the provision of services, how it is used, and the rights you have. Please read this policy carefully before using this site.

## 1. Scope of Information Collection

This site does not actively collect any information that could be used to identify you personally. When you visit this site, the server will automatically log certain technical information in accordance with industry practice, including:

- Visitor IP address
- Access time and request path
- Browser type and operating system information (User-Agent)

The aforementioned logs are used solely for site operations and security auditing, are not associated with other identity data, and will not be used to identify individuals.

## 2. Comment Feature

This site integrates the Waline comment system. If you choose to post a comment, the system will collect the following information:

- **Nickname** (required)
- **Email address** (optional, used only for comment reply notifications)
- **Personal website URL** (optional)
- **Comment content**

The aforementioned information is used solely to display comment content and send email notifications. This site will not use it for any other purpose, nor will it disclose, sell, or transfer it to any third party.

## 3. Cookies and Local Storage

To improve your browsing experience, this site may write Cookies to your browser or use LocalStorage to save your preferences, including but not limited to:

- Theme mode (light / dark)
- Language preference
- Sidebar collapse state
- Decryption credentials for encrypted articles (only when you actively choose "Remember password")

The above data is stored only on your local device and cannot be accessed or read by the server. You may clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services

This site uses the following third-party services in its operation. Each service provider adheres to its own privacy policy and compliance requirements:

- **Vercel** — Provides site hosting and CDN services, may collect access logs for traffic statistics
- **Waline** — Provides comment system services, independently deployed and operated
- **Cloudflare** — Provides CDN acceleration and security protection

For specific details on how third-party services collect and process data, please refer to their officially published privacy policies.

## 5. Data Security Commitment

This site makes the following commitments regarding data protection:

- Will not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information
- Will not use any of your data for commercial promotion or user profiling
- Will take reasonable technical and administrative measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or tampering

## 6. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be revised from time to time due to changes in laws and regulations, service modifications, or security considerations. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review it periodically to stay informed of the latest terms.
